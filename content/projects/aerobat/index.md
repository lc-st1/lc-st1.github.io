Aerobat is a flapping-wing robot developed by Dr. Alireza Ramezani and Dr. Eric Sihite in the Silicon Synapse Lab at Northeastern University. It features a bioinspired design based on bat physiology and utilizes a computational structure to drive a flapping gait with a single motor.

I designed and prototyped a piezoelectric slip-stick actuated mechanism embedded in the wing linkage structure to alter the wingtip trajectory during flapping. This produced a differential thrust between each wing, allowing for roll authority.
